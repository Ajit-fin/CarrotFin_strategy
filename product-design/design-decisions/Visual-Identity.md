## DD15: Digital Private Bank

**Decision:** Teal Cyan (`#3CDDC7`) M3 seed, dark-mode-first. Creative north star: "The Digital Private Bank" — editorial, high-end private banking aesthetic; quiet, expansive, infinitely intelligent. Key principles: intentional asymmetry, Glass card depth, left-weighted typography.

**Rationale:** Prior warm sage (`#6B8F71`) read as "garden app" in mockups — lacked financial authority.

**Assumptions:** C1

**Reversal trigger:** User testing reveals the premium aesthetic alienates S1-S2 segments or creates accessibility issues.

---

## Considered & Rejected

- **Warm sage palette (`#6B8F71`):** Calming but read as casual/lifestyle — positioning mismatch for financial advisor.
