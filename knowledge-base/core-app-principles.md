# CarrotFin: Core App Principles

> **Domain:** Design / Product  
> **Last updated:** 2026-05-07  
> **Status:** Companion file — pending review for merger into `design-principles.md`  
> **Staleness threshold:** 90 days (foundational)

> Seven atomic, non-overlapping principles. Together they are MECE across every dimension of the currently feasible user experience.

---

## 1. Context Over Convention
The interface adapts to who you are, not a pre-defined flow. Structured defaults exist but immediately yield to user context — a change in goal, life stage, or intent reshapes the experience in real time. There is no canonical home screen; the AI selects and arranges from an available palette of UI components to compose the right surface for this user, right now.

## 2. Voice + Screen, Optimised Together
The AI has two complementary output modes — voice and screen — and optimises which to use, and how, on every turn. Sometimes voice carries the insight while the screen shows the chart. Sometimes the screen alone is enough. The AI doesn't default to using both; it picks the combination that communicates most lucidly for this moment, respecting the user's context and the guardrails.

## 3. Structured Thinking, Not Just Answers
CarrotFin is a brainstorming partner, not a search engine. When a user's query is vague or complex, the AI provides structure to work through it — asking the right clarifying questions, showing the relevant trade-offs, and recommending a clear path. It doesn't return a list of options and walk away.

## 4. Firm on Principles, Empathetic in Delivery
If a user's intent violates a core financial safety principle (raiding an emergency fund, over-leveraging, panic-selling), the AI intervenes — clearly and without hedging. But it leads with the *why*, not a warning label. The correction is a fiduciary act delivered by a partner who is on your side.

## 5. Speed Is Trust
Voice and interaction latency must be aggressively minimized. A delayed response breaks the illusion of a present, intelligent advisor. Low latency is not a performance metric — it is the foundation on which conversational trust is built.

## 6. Read the Room
The AI calibrates its tone, pacing, and information density to the user's emotional state — not just their financial state. Anxiety gets reassurance before data. Excitement gets grounding without cold water. Overwhelm gets fewer components and simpler language. A great advisor reads the room before speaking.

## 7. Earn the Right to Know
Data is collected only when needed, immediately followed by value that explains why it was asked for. Complexity reveals itself progressively as the AI earns trust — not front-loaded in an onboarding form, not revealed all at once.
